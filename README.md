## Professional Summary

I am a machine learning engineer and researcher focused on decision-making models for cybersecurity, including Android malware detection under concept drift. 
My work also spans post-quantum cryptography in TEEs and confidential computing for software-defined vehicles. 
Research intern at Seoul National University, with first-author publications in CMES (SCIE Q1) and ACM SAC (BK21+ Outstanding Conference).

## Research Experience

### Seoul National University | [Security Optimization Research Lab](https://sor.snu.ac.kr/)

**Research Intern** · Seoul, Republic of Korea · Jun 2026 – Present  
Supervisor: Prof. Yunheung Paek; Mentor: Martin Kayondo

- Review mentor-assigned papers and deliver weekly presentations on **robot foundation models and vision-language-action systems**, with a current focus on **NVIDIA MIG and vGPU security**.

### Dankook University | [Computer Security and Operating Systems Lab](https://securesw.dankook.ac.kr/index.html)

**Undergraduate Research Assistant** · Yongin, Republic of Korea · Mar 2025 – Jul 2026  
Supervisor: Prof. Seong-je Cho

- Designed **SCAN** using Gaussian mixture clustering (GMM) and adaptive thresholds to address concept drift and class imbalance in Android malware detection; achieved **average accuracy and F1 scores above 91% across six years** and published the work as first author in CMES (SCIE Q1).
- Developed **API co-occurrence graph and community-based malware detection**, achieving an **F1 score above 90% over six years without retraining** and receiving the WDSC 2025 Best Paper Award. Extended the work with a mixture-of-experts model in **ALARM**, published at ACM SAC 2026 as first author.
- Implemented **split ML-KEM in OP-TEE on Arm TrustZone-A**, isolating secret-dependent operations in the Secure World and offloading public computation; reduced the **trusted code base by 90.8%** and **mean latency by 25.5%** relative to the baseline architecture. Received the Cyber Security Contest 1st Place Award and the KCC 2026 Outstanding Paper Award for the extended work.

**Research Intern** · Bloomington, IN, USA (Remote) · Oct 2025 – Jan 2026  
Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Hyungsub Kim
